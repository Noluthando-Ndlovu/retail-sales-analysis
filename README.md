# Retail Sales Analysis - South Africa

### Project Overview
Analyzed 2 years of retail sales data to identify trends, seasonal patterns, and regional performance across Gauteng, Western Cape & KZN.

### Key Insights
- Peak Season: December sales 2x higher than January
- Growth: 12% YoY growth from 2023 to 2024  
- Top Region: Gauteng contributes 45% of revenue
- Best Category: Electronics drives 60% of sales

- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib)
